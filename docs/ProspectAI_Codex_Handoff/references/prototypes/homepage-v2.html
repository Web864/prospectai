What does ProspectAI help me do?

ProspectAI is designed to help you interpret publicly available website signals and identify potential sales opportunities. You should validate individual insights before acting.

Who is ProspectAI for?

Freelancers, agencies, consultants and sales professionals who want stronger context for relevant prospecting.

Are the opportunities guaranteed?

No. A website signal is evidence to investigate, not a guarantee that a business needs a service or is ready to buy.
